Starshipped changes: converted glTF 1.0 to glTF 2.0; quantized vertices and
compressed with meshoptimizer; converted materials to PBR; added display
normalization, weathered surface shading, a recessed engine core, lighting,
and a solid holographic presentation. The studio variant adds 32-sample
per-vertex ambient occlusion; original geometry and textures are preserved.
